What Makes Me Different

Transformation by Design

How my approach stands apart from typical coaching

Most coaches help you set goals. I help you redesign how you see yourself. Here's what that actually means:

The Art of Alignment

I teach you to make decisions from your core values, not obligation or approval-seeking. Example: You stop saying yes to every meeting and protect your creative time instead. Your boundaries and brilliance coexist.

The Science of Creativity

Using NLP and Stanford's framework, I help you reframe your situation. Example: "I'm stuck" becomes "I'm spiraling upward with tools." "I'm chasing approval" becomes "I'm designing my impact." This rewires your nervous system.

The Soul of Connection

You don't spiral alone. In groups of 12, you bring real struggles: people-pleasing, guilt, over-functioning. You discover you're not broken. You're held by women in the same journey. That collective witness transforms what isolation never can.

The Framework That Ties It Together: The Golden Thread Spiral™

Most coaching is linear. Mine is spiral. You move through five stages: Awareness, Acceptance, Accountability, Action, Acknowledgement. Then you spiral back at a deeper level. Each cycle brings you closer to alignment. You ascend, not finish.

Meet Kim Herrlein

A story of loss, reinvention & generational healing

How collapse became clarity. How survival became design.

At 21, Kim's mother died at 44, trapped in silence and self-abandonment. That loss made her vow: her mother's story would not become hers. Years later, after a painful divorce, financial collapse, and the loss of her daughters' father to suicide, Kim stood in front of a mirror and realized: she didn't hate herself. She hated the life she had settled into.

That moment became her choice point.

Using the tools she now teaches, Kim rebuilt everything. Not just her circumstances, but her entire identity. As founder of Purveyors of Leisure and CEO of the Fainting Couch Boutique Hotel, she discovered her true gift wasn't in hospitality logistics. It was in guiding communities through transformation.

With a Master's in Psychology, NLP certification, and the hard-won wisdom of her own spiral, she created Insight Seeker to hold space for women to reconnect with themselves and rise into aligned leadership. She's a sought-after speaker and 3x Amazon best-selling co-author. But most importantly, she's a woman who knows what it feels like to be fragmented and then found again.
